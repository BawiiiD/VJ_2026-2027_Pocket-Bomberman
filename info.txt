Implemented features (basic part)
---------------------------------
- 4 screens: main menu, game, instructions and credits.
- 5 levels designed in Tiled (levels/*.tmx). In each one all enemies must be
  defeated to open the exit door. The last level has a boss; its door appears
  when the boss is defeated.
- Bomberman walks left/right and jumps (variable jump height).
- 3 enemies with different behaviours:
    * Walker (yellow): patrols its platform, turns at walls and edges.
    * Hopper (black): waits and jumps towards the player.
    * Chaser (pink): patrols slowly and runs after the player when it sees him.
- Touching an enemy costs a life unless Bomberman has the heart.
- 3 lives; losing all of them shows GAME OVER and returns to the menu.
- Bombs are always placed aligned with the tiles, float in the air, explode
  after 2.5 s and kill enemies and Bomberman. Explosions trigger other bombs.
- Bomberman can stand on his own bombs to reach higher places.
- Destructible bushes that may leave items.
- 4 items: Bomb Up, Fire Up, Heart (protects from one hit) and Speed Up.
- Time limit per level.
- Boss (tree) in level 5: throws seeds and birds, needs 5 hits.
- HUD: time left, lives, simultaneous bombs, explosion size, heart, speed,
  enemies left, stage and boss health.
- Cheats: G (god mode on/off), K (kill all enemies, opens the door),
  1-5 (load that level).

Instructions
------------
- LEFT / RIGHT: move
- UP or Z: jump
- SPACE or X: drop a bomb
- ESC: back to the main menu (quits the game from the main menu)
- In the menus: UP / DOWN to choose, ENTER to confirm.
