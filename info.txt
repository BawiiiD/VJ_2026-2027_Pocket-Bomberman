Implemented features
--------------------

Basic part
- 4 screens: main menu, game, instructions and credits.
- 5 levels designed with Tiled (levels/map0X.tmx + levels/level0X.txt). In
  each one all the enemies have to be defeated to open the exit door, and
  Bomberman has to walk into it. The levels are bigger than the screen and
  the camera follows Bomberman.
- Bomberman walks left and right and jumps. The jump is higher when the key
  is held and there is a short margin to jump just after leaving a ledge.
- 3 enemies with different behaviours:
    * Walker (pink): patrols its platform and turns around at walls and edges.
    * Hopper (orange): waits on the floor and then jumps towards Bomberman.
    * Chaser (yellow): patrols slowly and runs after Bomberman when it sees
      him in front of it on the same floor. It never falls from its platform.
  Touching an enemy costs a life, unless Bomberman has the heart.
- 3 lives. After dying the level starts again with the items Bomberman had
  when it started. Losing the 3 lives shows GAME OVER and goes back to the
  main menu.
- Bombs are always placed in the center of a tile and float in the air.
  They explode after 2.5 s (blinking faster at the end) and the fire kills
  enemies and Bomberman. A bomb hit by the fire explodes too (chain reaction).
- Bomberman can stand on his own bombs to reach higher places.
- Destructible bushes. Each level file says which bushes leave an item and
  which one (POWERUP lines).
- 4 items: Bomb Up (+1 bomb at the same time), Fire Up (+1 tile of
  explosion), Heart (survives one hit) and Speed Up (walks faster). They are
  kept from one level to the next.
- Time limit in each level. When it runs out Bomberman loses a life.
- Boss in level 5: a tree that walks from side to side, throws seeds in an
  arc and birds that fly towards Bomberman. It needs 5 explosions and
  attacks faster when it is almost defeated. When it dies the exit door
  appears.
- HUD: time left, lives, bombs at the same time, explosion size, heart,
  speed level, enemies left and the stage (or the boss health in level 5).
- Cheats:
    G      God mode on/off (Bomberman is invulnerable)
    K      Kills all the enemies (and the boss) and opens the exit door
    1 - 5  Loads that level

Level elements
- Spouts with a fence below them: the fence is a wall. When the fire of a
  bomb reaches the spout, the fence goes up and leaves the way open.
- Ray emitters: two blocks that wait, charge and shoot a ray between them
  for 1.5 s. The ray kills Bomberman and makes the bombs inside it explode.

Polish
- Sound (miniaudio): a different music for the menu, each world and the
  boss, jingles for stage clear, time up, game over and victory, and sound
  effects for the bombs, explosions, bushes, items, jumping, landing, deaths,
  the boss, the door opening, the time warning and the menus.
- Intro with the story of the original game (ENTER, SPACE or ESC skip it).
- Animations for every event: walking, jumping, falling, two different death
  animations (fire or enemy), celebrating at the door, enemies dying and
  blinking, bombs blinking faster before exploding, explosions that grow and
  shrink, bushes that blink when destroyed, floating items, the boss flashing
  red when hit and shaking when it dies, Bomberman blinking after losing the
  heart, the fence going up and the ray emitters charging.
- Transitions: stage title, stage clear, time up, game over and victory
  banners, and fades in the intro. The closed door is drawn darker until all
  the enemies are defeated, and the clock blinks in red in the last 30 s.


Instructions
------------
Goal: in each level defeat all the enemies with the bombs and then walk into
the exit door before the time runs out. In level 5 defeat the boss.

Controls
    LEFT / RIGHT   Move
    UP or Z        Jump (hold it to jump higher)
    SPACE or X     Drop a bomb
    ESC            Back to the main menu (in the main menu it quits the game)

Menus
    UP / DOWN      Choose an option
    ENTER          Confirm (ENTER, SPACE or ESC go back from the
                   instructions and credits screens)
    ENTER or SPACE Skip the intro and the game over / victory screens

Tips
- Bombs float: drop one, jump on it and drop another one to climb.
- Your own bombs can kill you, so step away before they explode.
- Bomb the spouts to open the fences, and cross the rays while they are off.
